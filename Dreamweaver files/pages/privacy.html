Privacy Policy

Browsing the portfolio

You can read the team profiles and watch their videos without signing in or providing personal details. The portfolio itself does not set cookies, store visitor profiles, or use advertising or analytics trackers.

Images and profile videos

The images and videos load directly from the portfolio’s files. When you play a profile video, your browser uses its built-in player. The portfolio does not record which videos you watch or how long you watch them.
